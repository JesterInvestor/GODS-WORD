# Farcaster Mini App - Required Assets

Your Mini App needs these image assets in the `public/` directory:

## Required Images

1. **`/og-image.png`** (Open Graph / Embed Preview)
   - Dimensions: 1200x800px (3:2 aspect ratio)
   - Used when sharing links to your app
   - Current location: Already exists

2. **`/icon-512.png`** (Splash Screen Icon)
   - Dimensions: 200x200px minimum (512x512px recommended)
   - Used as splash screen icon when launching Mini App
   - Should be your app logo/icon
   - Background: Transparent or matching `splashBackgroundColor`

## Action Required

Create or place these images in your `public/` folder:
- `public/og-image.png` ✅ (should already exist)
- `public/icon-512.png` ❌ (needs to be created)

The splash icon should represent your app well - consider using a Bible icon, cross, or your app's branding.
